Popular Questions

Quick answers to common queries

Browse our shop, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay via UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, or cash on delivery. Once confirmed, you'll receive an order summary via email/WhatsApp.
After your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking link via WhatsApp and email. You can also check your order status in the My Account section.
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Rupay), net banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible pin codes.
You can modify or cancel your order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Please contact our support team immediately for assistance.
Enter your promo code at checkout in the "Apply Coupon" field and click Apply. The discount will be reflected in your order total automatically.
We offer a 7-day return policy for damaged or incorrect items. If you receive a defective product, contact us with photos and your order ID. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.
Standard delivery within Tamil Nadu takes 2-4 business days. For other Indian states, delivery takes 5-7 business days. International orders to Singapore and other countries are delivered within 7-12 business days.
Yes, all our products are 100% certified organic. We source directly from verified organic farmers in Tamil Nadu. Our products carry NPOP and USDA Organic certifications.
Our Small Box is a curated selection of seasonal organic produce ideal for individuals or couples. The Big Box offers a larger variety and quantity, perfect for families. Both can be customized with your preferred vegetables, fruits, and spices.
Yes, we currently ship to Singapore and are expanding to more countries. For international orders, please contact our support team for shipping rates and delivery timelines. Minimum order values may apply for international shipments.
We welcome B2B partnerships with restaurants, hotels, retailers, and exporters. Visit our B2B page or contact us directly at support@vpnexues.com with your business details. Our team will reach out within 24 hours with customized pricing.
