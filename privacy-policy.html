🔒 Privacy Policy

How VPNexues Private Limited collects, uses and protects your personal data.

VPNexues Private Limited

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026 • DPDP Act 2023

Applies to: VPNexues App • Website vpnexues.com • Related Services

1. Introduction

VPNexues Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our app, website vpnexues.com, and related services. This policy is prepared with reference to the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

Company: VPNexues Private Limited

Address: No 312/11, Siva Towers, Perumal Malai Road, Narasothipatti, Salem, Tamil Nadu, 636004, India

Email: marketing@vpnexues.com | support@vpnexues.com

2. Information We Collect

Personal data you provide:

  • Phone number & name — for account registration and OTP login via Firebase Authentication.
  • Email address — for order updates and B2B communication.
  • Delivery address — house/door number, street, landmark, city, state, PIN, and recipient contact.
  • Location data — approximate or precise location (via device settings or manual map-pin) for delivery routing and serviceable zones.
  • B2B details (if applicable) — business name, GSTIN for invoicing and export documentation.
  • Payment information — VPNexues currently supports Cash on Delivery (COD). We do not directly collect or store card, UPI, or net-banking credentials on our servers.

Automatically collected data:

  • Device & connection info — OS version, IP address, browser type.
  • Push notification tokens — for order/dispatch alerts via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Account & authentication — create, manage, and secure your account via phone OTP.
  • Order fulfillment — process orders, deliver products, verify COD payments.
  • B2B trade — GST invoicing, export documentation (B2B users only).
  • Communication — order confirmations, dispatch updates, support responses.
  • Platform improvement — performance monitoring, fraud prevention.
  • Legal compliance — tax records, audit trails, statutory requirements.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share only with:

  • Google Firebase — phone OTP authentication and push notifications (Firebase Privacy Policy).
  • Supabase — secure PostgreSQL database and backend hosting (Supabase Privacy Policy).
  • Logistics partners — delivery names, addresses, and contact numbers (bound by confidentiality agreements).
  • Legal authorities — only when required by law, court order, or government authority.

5. Cookies, Notifications & Tracking

  • Session cookies — for login sessions and cart preferences.
  • Push notification tokens — used by Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver order and dispatch notifications.

Manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data — kept while your account is active; deleted upon verified account deletion request.
  • Transaction & tax records — retained as required by applicable Indian tax and commercial regulations.
  • OTP codes — processed through our authentication provider and not stored by VPNexues as account data.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may have rights regarding your personal data under applicable data protection laws, including rights relating to access, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent, grievance redressal, and nomination:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of marketing or non-essential processing anytime.
  • Grievance redressal — file a complaint if your issue is not resolved.
  • Nominate — designate someone to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

How to exercise your rights:

  • Email grievance@vpnexues.com with subject line "Privacy Rights Request".
  • Include your registered mobile number or email for identity verification.
  • Specify the right you wish to exercise (access, correction, deletion, etc.).
  • We will verify your identity and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Data Security

  • All data encrypted via TLS/HTTPS in transit.
  • Data stored with our service providers is protected using security measures provided by those services, including encryption at rest where applicable.
  • Payment security — VPNexues does not directly store card, UPI, or net-banking credentials on its servers.
  • Access restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We follow Reasonable Security Practices under the IT Act, 2000.

9. Third-Party Links & Services

Our website or application may contain links to third-party websites or services. VPNexues is not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of those services before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the service. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that we have collected such data, please contact grievance@vpnexues.com.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect upon posting at vpnexues.com/privacy-policy. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification.
